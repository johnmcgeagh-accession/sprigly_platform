# hook_patterns seed list — Stage 6

Ready for `packages/db` seed. Fields map to the migration: name, category, pattern
(structural template with {slots}), example (illustrative, original — not lifted copy),
formats (which post formats it suits). All active=true.

Categories: curiosity · contrarian · question · promise · pain · proof · story ·
identity · urgency · instructional

Format tags: reel, carousel. (Hooks generate for reels + carousels only; single
image and email are excluded by product decision.)

---

## Curiosity

1. **Curiosity gap** — curiosity — `The real reason {surprising outcome} — and it isn't {assumed cause}.`
   Example: "The real reason this sold out twice — and it isn't the fabric." — reel, carousel

2. **Withheld reveal** — curiosity — `We almost didn't {action}. Here's what changed our mind.`
   Example: "We almost didn't make this in green. Here's what changed our mind." — reel, carousel

3. **Unexpected pairing** — curiosity — `What {familiar thing} taught us about {your domain}.`
   Example: "What sourdough taught us about cutting linen." — reel, carousel

4. **Open loop** — curiosity — `There's one thing we never show on this account. Today we are.`
   Example: "There's one part of the studio we never film. Today we are." — reel

5. **Anomaly flag** — curiosity — `Something odd happens every time we {routine action}.`
   Example: "Something odd happens every time we restock the poplin shirt." — reel, carousel

6. **Behind the number** — curiosity — `{Specific number} {units}. Here's the story behind that number.`
   Example: "Forty-one metres of deadstock. Here's the story behind that number." — reel, carousel

## Contrarian

7. **Myth-bust** — contrarian — `Everyone says {common advice}. We do the opposite — here's why.`
   Example: "Everyone says post daily. We post nine times a month — here's why." — reel, carousel

8. **Unpopular opinion** — contrarian — `Unpopular opinion: {position that challenges category norms}.`
   Example: "Unpopular opinion: most 'sustainable' fabric claims don't survive a second question." — reel, carousel

9. **Stop doing X** — contrarian — `Stop {common practice}. Do {alternative} instead.`
   Example: "Stop washing linen like cotton. Do this instead." — reel, carousel

10. **Sacred cow** — contrarian — `{Beloved industry norm} is overrated. There, we said it.`
    Example: "Seasonal drops are overrated. There, we said it." — reel

11. **Quiet disagreement** — contrarian — `We were told {advice} when we started. Ignoring it was the best call we made.`
    Example: "We were told to chase trends when we started. Ignoring it was the best call we made." — reel, carousel

## Question

12. **Direct-address question** — question — `Have you ever {relatable moment in customer's life}?`
    Example: "Have you ever bought something twice because the first one never left the wash basket?" — reel, carousel

13. **Which-one poll** — question — `{Option A} or {option B}? Be honest.`
    Example: "Ochre or ivy green? Be honest." — reel, carousel

14. **Guess-the-answer** — question — `Can you guess {quantifiable fact about process/product}?`
    Example: "Can you guess how many pattern pieces are in one shirt?" — reel

15. **Self-audit question** — question — `When did you last {small behaviour tied to your value prop}?`
    Example: "When did you last repaired something instead of replacing it?" — reel, carousel

## Promise

16. **Numbered promise** — promise — `{N} {things} that {benefit} — number {k} is the one nobody does.`
    Example: "Five ways to style one shirt for a week — number four is the one nobody does." — carousel, reel

17. **Time-boxed payoff** — promise — `In the next {seconds}, you'll know exactly how to {outcome}.`
    Example: "In the next thirty seconds, you'll know exactly how to spot a well-made seam." — reel

18. **Complete guide** — promise — `Everything you need to know about {topic}, in one post. Save it.`
    Example: "Everything you need to know about caring for linen, in one post. Save it." — carousel

19. **Shortcut reveal** — promise — `The {timeframe} version of {complex thing}.`
    Example: "The two-minute version of how a garment gets costed." — reel, carousel

20. **Do-this-get-that** — promise — `Do {one small thing} and {specific improvement} follows.`
    Example: "Change one washing habit and your knits last twice as long." — reel, carousel

## Pain

21. **Relatable pain** — pain — `You know that feeling when {specific frustration}? Let's fix it.`
    Example: "You know that feeling when a new top bobbles after two wears? Let's fix it." — reel, carousel

22. **Silent struggle** — pain — `Nobody talks about {hidden difficulty}. So we will.`
    Example: "Nobody talks about how hard sizing is for small brands. So we will." — reel, carousel

23. **Cost of inaction** — pain — `{Avoided task} is costing you more than you think.`
    Example: "That drawer of 'almost right' basics is costing you more than you think." — carousel

24. **Mistake confession** — pain — `We got {thing} badly wrong. Here's what it taught us.`
    Example: "We got our first production run badly wrong. Here's what it taught us." — reel, carousel

## Proof

25. **Receipts open** — proof — `{Specific result, plainly stated}. Here's exactly how.`
    Example: "Sold out in nineteen hours. Here's exactly how." — reel, carousel

26. **Before/after** — proof — `{Starting state} → {end state}. The middle is the interesting bit.`
    Example: "Flat sketch → finished garment. The middle is the interesting bit." — reel, carousel

27. **Third-party voice** — proof — `A customer said {short paraphrased sentiment}. We want to unpack that.`
    Example: "A customer said this shirt 'ended her Sunday ironing'. We want to unpack that." — reel, carousel

28. **Live test** — proof — `We put {claim} to the test on camera.`
    Example: "We put the 'no-crease' claim to the test on camera." — reel

## Story

29. **In-media-res** — story — `{Drop straight into mid-scene, present tense}.`
    Example: "The boxes arrive at 7am and the whole plan changes." — reel

30. **Origin fragment** — story — `{Time marker}, {founder} {small concrete scene that started it all}.`
    Example: "Three summers ago, Sally cut up her favourite worn-out shirt to see how it was made." — reel, carousel

31. **Day-in-the-life** — story — `{Time} on a {day}. This is what {role/process} actually looks like.`
    Example: "6:40 on a Tuesday. This is what a restock morning actually looks like." — reel

32. **Turning point** — story — `Everything was fine until {inflection moment}.`
    Example: "Everything was fine until the fabric mill closed with our order inside." — reel

## Identity

33. **POV** — identity — `POV: you're {person in audience's aspirational/relatable situation}.`
    Example: "POV: you're the friend whose outfit everyone asks about, quietly." — reel

34. **This-is-for-you** — identity — `If you {specific behaviour/preference}, this one's for you.`
    Example: "If you'd rather own five perfect things than fifty average ones, this one's for you." — reel, carousel

35. **Us-vs-the-category** — identity — `We're not a {category label} brand. Here's what we are instead.`
    Example: "We're not a fast-fashion brand doing slow-fashion marketing. Here's what we are instead." — reel, carousel

36. **Insider reveal** — identity — `Things {insiders} know that {outsiders} don't.`
    Example: "Things pattern cutters know that shoppers don't." — carousel, reel

## Urgency

37. **Quiet scarcity** — urgency — `{Small batch fact}, and when it's gone it's gone — here's why we won't remake it.`
    Example: "Sixty pieces, and when they're gone they're gone — here's why we won't remake them." — reel, carousel

38. **Window closing** — urgency — `You've got {timeframe} before {change}. Use it well.`
    Example: "You've got one week before the price of this fabric changes everything. Use it well." — reel

## Instructional

39. **Watch-me-do-it** — instructional — `Watch us {process} from start to finish — no cuts.`
    Example: "Watch us press and finish one shirt from start to finish — no cuts." — reel

40. **Common-mistake fix** — instructional — `You're probably {doing task} wrong. Two changes fix it.`
    Example: "You're probably storing knitwear wrong. Two changes fix it." — reel, carousel

41. **Checklist open** — instructional — `Before you {common action}, check these {N} things.`
    Example: "Before you buy 'organic cotton', check these three things." — carousel

42. **One-thing rule** — instructional — `If you only remember one thing about {topic}, make it this.`
    Example: "If you only remember one thing about fit, make it this." — reel, carousel

---

Seeding notes for Claude Code:
- Store `pattern` with the {slot} placeholders intact — the generation prompt should show
  the model the structure, not just the example.
- Examples are illustrative in a fashion/founder-led register (nearest to the pilot
  client) but are original copy; they exist to show the pattern filled in, and the
  model should be instructed to imitate the *structure*, never the example's content.
- Category + formats are the selection keys; keep selection logic reading `active=true`
  only, so retiring a pattern is an UPDATE, not a deploy.
- Deliberate spread: no category dominates, and every pattern tagged carousel works as
  a first-slide text hook as well as a spoken opener.
