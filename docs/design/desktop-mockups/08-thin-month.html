A thin month at 1440 — solved, not padded

Two planned posts on a screen with room for thirty. On a phone the day view is invariant to month size and the problem barely exists; at 1440 the grid is the one region that displays emptiness, and it displays it in five rows.

Three things carry it, and none of them is padding. First, the two right-hand columns — the day and the conversation — are invariant to month size and hold 664 of the 1440, so the visibly empty region is one column of four. Second, the month summary opens by default on a thin month, which fills that column with the month’s real derivation: the mix, what came from her, what we assumed. Third, the conversation’s opening turn is the thin-month acknowledgement rather than the standard framing — it is the one state where the agent has something specific to say first.

What is not done. No ghost cells, no placeholder slots, no greyed “add a post here” on twenty-eight empty days, and no error styling — a thin month is not a failure, and dressing it as one would make a client distrust a month that may be exactly right. The rationale names the gap in words instead.

Provenance

  • Every line of the panel is month-summary.md §1’s thin variant, verbatim — the same derivation over the first two of ivy-t’s live September rows.
  • The card is position 0: 1 September, carousel, pillar A Supportive Friend, Always By Your Side, title Here for you, always (beat-detail.md §3). The reel on the 2nd is the backlog beat, which is why the panel says “1 idea you gave us in July”.
  • The thin-data rationale sentence is the mobile spec §9.2’s, verbatim.
  • The card carries no time: this beat is on no series, and ivy-t has no readable stored posting time for an ordinary slot. An absent field produces no line rather than a guess.

Decisions this frame carries

  • The summary opens by default only when the month is thin. On a full month it opens closed, because thirty posts are their own argument. Two are not, and the panel is where the argument lives.
  • The acknowledgement moved into the conversation. On the phone it sits at the foot of the day, after the client has read what there is. Here the conversation is permanently on screen and speaks first, which is a better home for it than a line at the bottom of a short column — and it puts the invitation next to the field that answers it.
  • The grid keeps its full height. Shrinking the calendar to fit two posts would be padding in reverse: it would make a thin month look like a small month, and the client would have no way to see that twenty-eight days are free.
  • The Generate pill stays exactly where it is. A thin month is still a month you may approve, and the modal counts what is actually there.
