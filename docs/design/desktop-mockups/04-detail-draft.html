Detail panel — a planned post, and the evidence it is made of

The committed panel’s structure with the three parts that genuinely do not exist yet removed: no tabs (there is no caption, hook or script until the month is generated), no Shape (a rewrite of an empty field is a paid no-op), no Copy (nothing to put on a clipboard). Move has no time, because {op:'move'} writes a date and the draft route has no posting-time op to save one with.

What it keeps is the part that matters most on a draft: the reason, behind the insights icon. On a committed post that is a nice-to-have; on a planned one it is the whole proposition, because the client is being asked to approve a slot and the evidence for the slot is what they are approving.

The format control is visible here and not inside Shape, and that is a consequence of the ruling rather than an exception to it. The ruling’s own reason is consequences: a planned post has no caption, hook or script to strand, and it has no Shape mode to put the control in.

Decisions this frame carries

  • The empty state is a sentence, not three dead tabs. “Nothing written yet / This slot is held for you. The words arrive when you say the month is ready.” — the shipped copy, unchanged.
  • Two action buttons, not three. Move and Delete fill the row; Shape is absent rather than disabled, because a disabled control still asks to be understood.
  • The evidence panel is 320px here and 350px on the phone, so the long lines wrap in almost exactly the same places. That is the argument for translating the panel rather than redesigning it at width: the lines were measured against this measure.
