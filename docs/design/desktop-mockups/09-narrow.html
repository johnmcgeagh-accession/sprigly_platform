Narrow desktop — 1024, and what the band between 1080 and 1280 does

The rule, stated once. Below 1080px the surface is the mobile shell, unchanged — the existing PlanRoot breakpoint is not moved, because it is already tested and both e2e projects are pinned either side of it. A tablet in portrait gets the phone surface, which is a reviewed design tested at 375 and 320, rather than a squeezed desktop nobody has looked at.

Between 1080 and 1279 the plan region stacks: the month grid above, the day column (or the detail panel) below, scrolling together. The rail collapses to icons and the dock narrows from 344 to 320. At or above 1280 the month and the day sit side by side, which is exception 2 in full.

What does not change at any width. The conversation is docked and present. It never becomes a tab, a rail item, or a button that summons a panel — that is the whole of exception 1, and a region that disappears when the window narrows is not persistent, it is merely usually visible.

A — 1024 × 820 · the detail panel open, the conversation still docked

The month is above and the post is below, both at the plan region’s full 588px, and the conversation has not moved. The detail panel is wider here than at 1440 — 588px against 320 — which is a property of stacking, not a second design: the panel takes whatever the region gives it. The rail is icons only, and its late count becomes a dot.

B — 1024 × 820 · the reshape moment, the frame this breakpoint has to survive

This is the layout-sensitive one, and it is the reason the dock does not collapse below 1280. The whole point of the docked conversation is that the sentence and its consequence are visible together; a rail-tab that expands over the month would put them back on separate screens, which is the phone’s compromise and not a narrower desktop’s. Both are on screen, which is the claim this breakpoint has to keep. What the stack does cost is the day column beside the grid: the day the change lands on is below the fold here, so the client sees where it goes on the calendar and reads the post itself one scroll later.

The breakpoints, as a rule the build can implement

  • < 1080px — the mobile shell (PlanShell), unchanged. The existing matchMedia('(min-width: 1080px)') fork in PlanRoot.tsx stays exactly as it is.
  • 1080–1279px — desktop shell, plan region stacked, rail collapsed to 68px, dock 320px.
  • ≥ 1280px — desktop shell, month and day side by side, rail 196px with labels, dock 344px.
  • The rail’s collapse is also a manual control at every width — the existing rail-toggle, carried forward. Above 1280 it is the client’s choice; below it, it is the default.

Checked at this width

  • No horizontal overflow anywhere in either frame — probed with scrollWidth against clientWidth on every element inside the frame, not eyeballed.
  • The month grid’s cells are 84px at 1024 against 69px at 1440, so the day numeral and its pips have more room, not less. The grid is the region that gains from stacking.
  • The detail panel’s caption measure goes from ~288px to ~556px. Longer lines for the same words: acceptable for a caption, and the reason the panel does not fix its own width.
