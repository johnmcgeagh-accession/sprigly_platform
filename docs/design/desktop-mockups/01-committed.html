Committed month — the shell

Earl of East, October 2026, after approval: ten posts, real captions, one still on its way. This is the whole desktop shell in its resting state — left rail, month grid and day column side by side, and the conversation docked and present without being opened.

Four columns, and the arithmetic is fixed: rail 196 · month 512 · day 320 · dock 344, with 24px gutters and a 20px column gap. 196 + 24 + 512 + 20 + 320 + 24 + 344 = 1440. The two columns that are invariant to how full the month is — the day and the conversation — hold 664px of it, which is what stops a thin month reading as a broken screen (mockup 8).

The grid is a density map, not a second content surface. It carries the phone's own dot grammar at a larger size: a filled chrome dot per post, a ring for a post still being written, and the selected day as the same accent-650 circle the week strip uses. What the dots are is the column beside it. That is round 6's P6 ruling — tap a day, stay where you are, read what it holds — with the “beneath” become “beside”.

What is on this screen, and where each thing came from

  • The ten posts, their dates, formats, pillars and statuses — docs/reports/build-d-approval-phase2.md §1, the dogfood run. Zero structure drift across generation.
  • The reel’s caption excerpt — verbatim from the same report. The 1 October single post’s caption text is not recorded (only its length, 274 characters), so the card states the length rather than showing invented copy — the same reconstruction the phone mockups made.
  • The 4 October ring — that carousel is generation_failed in the report. Client-facing that is on its way, never a retry (G4), and the marker is a different shape so it survives greyscale.
  • Posting times — the PostingTimes contract’s own documented example values (packages/engine/src/types.ts: launch 6am, morning 7am). Not a stored client config; labelled here as it is in the phone set.
  • “2 late” on Tasks — derived from the real step_templates rows (0067) instantiated against the real post dates, with today at 1 October. Which steps are ticked is recorded nowhere; mockup 7 states that.

Decisions this frame carries

  • No global “Add to your plan” button. It is retired here as it was on the phone: adding is the per-day slot, and briefing is the conversation. That control was also the surface’s one real accessibility defect — coral text on white at 3.03:1, which is what three of the eighteen standing desktop e2e failures are reporting.
  • No Timeline, no Notes, no Approvals rail item. The grid and the day column supersede Timeline; the thread and the month summary supersede Notes; proposals are approved on the turn that raised them, inside the dock. The spec states each removal and what absorbs it.
  • The rail is light, not the pre-redesign dark. White rail, grey working area, white dock. A dark rail opposite a white conversation panel puts the two loudest values at the outside edges and leaves the month — the thing the client came for — as the quietest region on the screen.
  • The conversation is present and empty, not hidden. Its first turn is the agent’s framing sentence, which is exactly how the phone’s sheet opens; on desktop it simply never had to be opened.
