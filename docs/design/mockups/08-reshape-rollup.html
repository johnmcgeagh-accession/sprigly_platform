Reshape moment — the expanded rollup

What the expand control on the summary chip slides up. Real clients paste briefs, not sentences: a document-shaped paste is split into its instructions, each routed through the unmodified classifier, and the whole thing comes back as one receipt with one line per segment. Round 2 moves that list off the day and into a panel, so the chip above the week stays 48px whether the client said one thing or fourteen.

Where the content comes from

  • All fourteen segments, their kinds and their outcomes are the acceptance fixture in docs/reports/brief-decomposer.md §COMMIT 4 — Sally’s real August brief through the real decomposer, asserted at 8 month-scoped applications, 6 evergreen filings, exactly 1 next-cycle deferral, and one receipt line per segment.
  • 14, not 13. The round-1 brief said “We found 13 things”; the failure report describes ~13 distinct intents and the decomposer’s fixture splits the brief into 14. The surface renders receipt.segmentCount, so it says 14.
  • The chip’s counts are derived, the panel’s lines are reconstructions. “8 applied · 6 saved” follows directly from the fixture’s assertions. The per-item diff lines are phrased from the recorded post counts and dates — the rendered lines[] strings are not stored.
  • Rescuing one item currently loses the rollup. The existing add_to_month op returns a single receipt, which replaces the rollup in the panel (brief-decomposer.md, “Found, and left unfixed” §2). The mockup shows the intended behaviour — item promoted in place, panel kept — and the spec lists it as a gap.
  • Vocabulary. The fixture’s internal language is “beats”; every line here says posts. See the spec’s terminology table.
