Reshape moment — the expanded rollup

What the expand control slides up. Real clients paste briefs, not sentences: a document-shaped paste is split into its instructions, each routed through the unmodified classifier, and the whole thing returns as one receipt with one line per segment.

The type scale inside the panel was too flat in round 2 — 12.5, 13 and 13.5px, a 1.1 ratio across three roles that do different jobs. The segment text now leads at 15px with its disclosure dropped to a 12px uppercase label, so the eye can find the instruction before the machinery.
