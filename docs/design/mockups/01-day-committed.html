  • Two titles are the assembler’s deterministic fallback form because the report elides those beats’ phrased titles.
  • Posting times are the PostingTimes contract’s documented example values (packages/engine/src/types.ts). No reader surfaces a client’s stored posting_times — spec gap 1, which round 2 widens: the detail sheet’s date+time picker needs that field writable, not just readable.

The “on its way” treatment has a dependency

  • Bounded retry exists — GENERATION_JOB_OPTIONS is {attempts: 3, backoff: exponential 5s}, and generation_failed is stamped only once BullMQ has nothing left to retry (consumer.ts isFinalAttempt).
  • The daily sweep does not. A repeatable scheduler-tick runs at 05:00 Europe/London and already carries one sweep (sweepUnsentPlanReady) — so the hook exists and a failed-generation sweep is a sibling of something real. But today generation_failed is explicitly terminal: “nothing retries it, the post is client-visible with its error” (plan-ready.ts).
  • There is no operator surface for it either — generation_failed appears nowhere in admin/src. Removing the client’s retry affordance without both of those strands the post. Recorded as a blocking dependency in the spec, not a detail.
