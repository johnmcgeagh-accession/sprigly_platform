Month overview

Reached by tapping the small-caps month label. A compact grid with dot density per day — coral for draft beats, slate for committed posts — over a month-name row that doubles as the month picker: chevrons and swipe move between months, and a month holding a draft carries a badge. Tapping any day drops back into that day’s view. This one control replaces the month pills and closes the “October doesn’t show” class of bug: every month the client has is named on screen, one tap away, instead of two blind “next” presses (round-two-email-and-surface.md §B4).

Where the content comes from, and what it needs that doesn’t exist

  • The ten dated beats are Earl of East’s approved October list (build-d-approval-phase2.md §1); the 4 October failure is the real Bedrock timeout recorded there. Frame A shows them pre-approval, Frame B post-approval — same dates, because approval writes no structural change (0/10 drift).
  • Three months in the picker — [Aug, Sep, Oct] is the cycle list observed in round-two-email-and-surface.md. The pills name the month only; no per-month post counts are claimed, because September’s is not recorded anywhere.
  • The draft badge on a month pill has no field behind it. loadCycleList already knows which cycles hold a reviewable draft — it uses cyclesWithReviewableDraft() to decide whether a draft-only cycle qualifies for the menu at all — but CycleSummary does not carry that fact to the client. One boolean, listed in the spec’s gap table.
  • Dot density across a whole month needs one read that does not exist. GET /api/plan returns the viewed cycle’s posts; GET /api/plan/draft returns one cycle’s beats. Painting dots for a month you have not opened means a per-day count endpoint, or accepting that only the viewed month is dotted. Also in the gap table.
