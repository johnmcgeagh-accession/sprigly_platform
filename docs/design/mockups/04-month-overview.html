Month view — a peer, not an overlay

Reached and left through the Week | Month switcher on the title row. It is not a modal, it has no ✕, and it keeps the same header, the same tab bar and the same FAB as the week view. Tapping a day switches back to Week on that day.

The legend is gone. A calendar that needs a printed key has an encoding problem, and the round-2 version shipped one. The two states that need distinguishing now differ in shape — a filled dot is a post, a ring is a post still being written — and the one-line summary under the grid names the exception in words instead.
