What is recorded, and what is not

  • Frame A’s single diff line is deliberately thin. The Build D report records the classifier’s output, the ten approved beats, and zero structure drift — it does not record the receipt lines that application produced. So the strip shows only the delta the report does establish: the 1 October launch beat. No “replaced” line is shown for Earl of East, because none is recorded, and a launch arc that balances its removes against its adds would have produced some.
  • Frame B is a verbatim receipt from docs/reports/ivy-t-rehearsal-failures.md — three Added and three Replaced lines, with their real dates.
  • The “Just changed” marker already exists — changedIds on the receipt drives it today, held in memory and gone on reload. Deliberate: a highlight has done its job by the time you look away.
  • “Say it again and we’ll undo this one” has no mechanism behind it. Undo is one slot, in memory, and covers structural mutations — not a whole applied intent. Listed in the spec’s gap table; the copy is shown so the shape of the promise is reviewable, not because it ships.
