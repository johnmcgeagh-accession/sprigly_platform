Round 3 — what changed

  • Colour is tokens. Every value reads a --t-* custom property with the same name app/src/lib/theme.ts injects. These files demonstrate the active theme, Teal v1; changing the twelve lines in the stylesheet's THEME block repaints all ten pages, which is the point of showing it this way.
  • The vivid rule. White on accent-600 measures 2.49:1 and fails both floors. Rather than darkening the fill we flip the ink: chrome-deep on accent-600, 5.88:1. That keeps the brightest tier on the selected day, the FAB, badges, the summary chip and primary buttons. The most saturated moment in the product is the voice-sheet waveform, where accent-500 on chrome-deep reaches 7.86:1.
  • No serif anywhere. Round 2 kept Fraunces for the wordmark and month title; it was never loaded, so both rendered as Georgia. Everything is the native stack now.
  • Bottom tab bar (Plan | Tasks, built for 3–4), Today, and a Week | Month switcher that makes the month a peer view rather than an overlay.
  • Icons v2 — clapperboard, stacked squares, framed image — screenshot-tested at 17px before adoption. The clapperboard took three attempts.
  • Mic is the FAB; approval is a labelled pill. Round 2 had these the wrong way round.
  • Action rows are icon-only, 44px+, with aria-labels.
