Round 2 — what changed since the phone review

  • Type. UI and body text use the native stack, which renders as SF Pro Text on the device. Fraunces survives as two brand moments: the Sprigly wordmark and the month title. This supersedes round 1’s Inter-for-UI rule.
  • Format is an icon — film strip, stacked squares, single square — never a word chip. The word survives as the icon’s tooltip and its screen-reader label.
  • No “beat” anywhere client-facing. Draft items are planned posts; the draft framing carries the provisional meaning.
  • No retry or failed state. A post still being written reads as on its way. Retrying is system behaviour; a failure that survives it belongs to the operator. This has a dependency — see the spec.
  • The account chip is gone, and comes back when there is something behind it to open.
  • One lateral mechanism for months — the ‹ › arrows. The overview opens from a button-shaped month control with a chevron.
  • One day at a time. The week strip selects; the panel below shows the selected day and nothing else.

Reading these

  • Every frame is 390px wide — an iPhone 14/15 logical viewport. Frames sit side by side on a desktop screen; on a narrow screen they stack and drop the rounded shell.
  • Nothing is interactive. States are rendered as they are being reviewed. The only live behaviour anywhere is the native <details> expansion in the rollup.
  • The brand system is reproduced from app/tailwind.config.ts, not re-invented: coral 600 #E8705F / 700 #C4523F / 800 #8A3323 / 100 #FADDD6, brand slate #334155, muted #5C6470, line #8F9296, canvas #F2F3F5. Coral is never used for small text; coral text and coral icons appear only on coral-100. White on coral-600 appears only at 15px/600 and above.
