Where the content comes from

  • All fourteen segments, their kinds and their outcomes are the acceptance fixture in docs/reports/brief-decomposer.md §COMMIT 4 — Sally’s real August brief through the real decomposer, asserted at 8 month-scoped applications, 6 evergreen filings, exactly 1 next-cycle deferral, and one receipt line per segment.
  • 14, not 13. The prompt for this design session says “We found 13 things”; the failure report describes ~13 distinct intents, and the decomposer’s own fixture splits the brief into 14. The number on screen is whatever receipt.segmentCount is — shown here as 14 because that is what the fixture produces.
  • The per-item diff lines are reconstructions, not stored strings. The fixture records beat counts and dates per segment (“applied: 4 beats · 1 deferred (4 Sep)”), not the rendered lines[]. They are phrased here in the form the existing diff renderer uses.
  • Rescuing one item currently loses the rollup. The existing add_to_month op returns a single receipt, which replaces the rollup in the panel (brief-decomposer.md “Found, and left unfixed” §2). The mockup shows the intended behaviour — item promoted in place, rollup kept — and the spec lists it as a gap.
