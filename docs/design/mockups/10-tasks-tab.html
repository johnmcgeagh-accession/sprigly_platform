What this is and is not

  • Not a redesign of Tasks. The live checklist has its own model — steps with lead days, at-risk derivation and a progress ring (app/src/lib/checklist.ts). This stub shows the nav pill working; the Tasks surface itself is out of scope for this brief.
  • The task rows are illustrative and labelled as such. Real steps come from PostStepView and are generated per post; no client's actual step list is recorded in the reports, so these are shaped like real ones rather than copied from one.
  • Insights does not exist and is not drawn. The pill is built to take a fourth segment; nothing behind it is designed.
