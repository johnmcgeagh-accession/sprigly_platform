Q: What does working with Sprigly look like?
A: Three steps. We start with a discovery call — twenty minutes, no pitch — to work out what's eating your time. Then we run a Sprigly Audit, where we look at how you actually do the work using your own past emails, documents and replies. From there we build, and you're live in two to three weeks. After that you brief work in by email or Slack and it comes back, usually within the hour.

Q: How do I give Sprigly work day-to-day?
A: You brief it the way you'd brief a colleague — a short email or Slack message. No forms, no special syntax. The work comes back to you to review, normally within an hour. Nothing goes out the door until you're happy with it.

Q: How is this different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?
A: Off-the-shelf AI doesn't know how your business works, so it gives you generic output you then have to rewrite. Sprigly is trained on your own past work — your emails, your documents, the way you actually answer clients. What comes back sounds like you and follows how you already do things.

Q: What can Sprigly actually do for my business?
A: It takes on the repeatable work that eats your week: drafting replies to common questions, first drafts of documents and proposals, researching prospects before a call, putting reports together. The rule of thumb is simple. If it's work you've done many times and could explain to a new hire, Sprigly can usually take it on.

Q: What kind of work does it replace?
A: The middle layer. Not the strategic thinking or the client relationships — the execution that sits underneath them. Typically it replaces the work of a mid-level hire: the drafting, the research, the formatting, the first passes. You keep the judgement, it does the legwork.

Q: Can you give me some use cases for my business?
A: A few common ones. For agencies and consultancies: proposals and scoping documents from a short brief, call notes turned into follow-ups. For estate and lettings agents: answering routine enquiries, drafting property descriptions, chasing paperwork. For financial advisers: first drafts of client reviews, summarising research, handling common client questions. The best place to start is wherever you spend time on work you've done a hundred times.

Q: How many workstreams should I start with?
A: Start with one or two. Pick the work that's most repetitive and eats the most time — that's where you'll feel it fastest. You can add more once the first ones are running. One solid workstream live beats five half-built ones.

Q: How big does my business need to be?
A: Sprigly is built for founder-led firms, roughly five to thirty people. You need enough repeatable work to be worth automating, and a founder who still touches the work closely enough to know how it should be done. If you're starting to think about your next hire to handle the overflow, that's usually the right moment.

Q: What does Sprigly cost?

The way to think about it: it's there to replace real work and save you a future hire, so weigh it against what that hire would cost you.

Q: What is the monthly fee for?
A: The roughly £300 a month covers running the tooling — the platform, the models, and keeping your workstreams live and working. The setup fee is the one-off build. The monthly is what keeps it running.

Q: Why is pricing a range and not a fixed number?
A: Because the work isn't one size. Setup depends on how many workstreams you're automating and how involved each one is. One straightforward workstream sits at the lower end, several more involved ones at the higher end. We work out exactly where you land on the discovery call.

A: After the discovery call and audit, most clients are live in two to three weeks. The audit is the part that takes a little time, since that's where we learn how you work. Once it's done, the build is quick.
